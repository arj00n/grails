What it does

  • Grid

    Twenty thousand pictures, still smooth.

  • Canvas

    Drag pictures into piles. Leave gaps where you haven’t decided.

  • Preview

    Press space to give one picture the whole screen.

  • Boards

    Paste an Are.na, Pinterest or X link. Each board becomes a collection.

  • Search

    Find by name, tag or the words inside a picture. Tags come on their own.

  • Teams

    Share a library with your team. Send one link.

Your library

Yours to set up
Keep your library on your Mac, a drive or Dropbox. Share it with a team or keep it to yourself.
Yours to keep
It’s a plain folder you can move, back up or take anywhere. We never see it.
No account
No sign-up, no server, no tracking.
