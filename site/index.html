What it does

One place for the pictures you collect, kept as files on your Mac.

  • Grid

    Square or masonry tiles that stay smooth with 20,000 pictures. Pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom.

  • Canvas

    Arrange pictures in clusters on an open canvas, per collection or tag. ⌘2 switches to it.

  • Preview

    Space opens a picture full screen, with every detail beside it. Swipe or use the arrow keys.

  • Import boards

    Paste Are.na, Pinterest or X links. Each board becomes a collection; whole Pinterest boards are read inside the app.

  • Save from the browser

    Right-click or ⌥-click any image to save it, with the optional Chrome extension.

  • Tags

    Tag by hand, or let Grails tag new pictures on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Search

    ⌘F searches names, tags, notes and sources. ⌘K jumps to any collection, tag or command.

  • Shared libraries

    Keep a library in a Google Drive or Dropbox folder. Teammates’ saves appear within seconds.

  • Export

    Send a view as one HTML file or a PDF that anyone can open.

How it works

  1. Download

    Open the disk image and drag Grails to Applications.

  2. Open

    Press Start, then choose Import boards or Start empty.

  3. Paste your boards

    Paste links from Are.na, Pinterest or X. The pictures arrive in your library.

Your pictures stay yours

On your Mac
A library is a folder, Grails Library.grails in Pictures. Control-click it ▸ Show Package Contents: every picture is a plain file.
No account
Nothing to sign up for. There is no Grails server, so your pictures never pass through one.
No analytics
The app and this site don’t track what you do.
Tags on device
Auto-tagging runs on your Mac.
Online when you ask
Grails goes online to fetch what you import or save: a board, a picture, a link preview.
The extension
It talks only to Grails on your Mac (127.0.0.1). Privacy

Install

  1. Open Grails-0.2.0.dmg and drag Grails to Applications.
  2. Open Grails. The first time, macOS says it can’t verify the app: Grails is signed ad-hoc and not yet notarised by Apple.

    Control-click Grails in Applications ▸ Open ▸ Open. If macOS doesn’t offer Open: System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway.

  3. After that it opens like any other app.

Invite links

Keep a library in a shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder and your team works in it together. Copy Invite Link gives a link like:

https://grails.arjoon.xyz/open#lib=01J9…&name=Team%20Inspo

Opening it hands the link to Grails, which asks for the synced folder and checks it is the same library. Links to a collection, tag or picture work the same way. Everything after the # stays in your browser: this site never receives it.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No account, no trial, no subscription.
What does it need?
A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
What can I keep in it?
Pictures, GIFs, videos and links. A link gets its preview image or a snapshot of the page.
Where are my pictures?
In a library folder on your Mac, ~/Pictures/Grails Library.grails unless you choose another place, such as a synced folder.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Your library opens and works without a connection; importing boards and link previews need one.
Can my team share a library?
Yes, through a shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder. There is no server to run. Send teammates an invite link.
Which browsers does the extension support?
Chrome and other Chromium browsers, such as Arc, Brave and Edge.
Does Grails collect data?
No. There are no analytics and nothing is sent to us.
How do I update?
Download the new version here and replace the app in Applications. Your library stays where it is.
