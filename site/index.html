FAQ

How is Grails different from Are.na or Pinterest?

Those keep boards in the cloud. Grails imports them onto your Mac as a fast local library you own. Share with a normal synced folder.

How is it different from Eagle or Atlas?

Same idea: a native Mac reference library. Grails is free and open source, made for a shared team folder, and imports Are.na, Pinterest, and X boards.

How does team sharing work with no server?

The library is a plain folder. Put it on Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud, invite the team, and each person opens that folder in Grails.

Can I bring what I already have?

Yes. Paste a board link to import it as a collection. Files stay on your disk.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no trial.
