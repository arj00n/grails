What it does

  • Grid.

    Twenty thousand pictures, still smooth. Zoom out for the pile, zoom in for one.

  • Canvas.

    Drag pictures into piles. Leave gaps where you haven’t decided.

  • Preview.

    Space gives one picture the whole screen. Swipe to the next.

  • Boards.

    Paste an Are.na, Pinterest or X link. Each board becomes a collection.

  • Tags and search.

    Tag by hand or let Grails do it on your Mac. ⌘F finds, ⌘K jumps.

  • Teams.

    Keep a library in a shared Drive or Dropbox folder. Send an invite link.

Yours

A folder
Your library is a plain folder, on your Mac, a drive or Dropbox. Nothing is locked in.
No account
No sign-up, no server, no analytics.
Native
Built with Apple’s own frameworks, not a web wrapper.

Install

  1. Open Grails-0.2.0.dmg and drag Grails to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can’t verify the app, because it isn’t notarised yet: System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway.
