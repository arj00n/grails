Save to Grails

Privacy, last updated 5 October 2026.

In one line

The extension sends what you save only to the Grails app on your own Mac. Nothing goes to the developer or to any server.

What it handles

  • The page address, title and picture or video address of what you choose to save, and for Pinterest boards the pins of the board you ask it to import.
  • It hands these to the Grails app running on your Mac, over a connection that never leaves your computer (127.0.0.1).
  • It remembers, in your browser's own extension storage, a connection code that Grails issued, the port Grails listens on, and your last few saved items so it can list them. Nothing else.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, usage data or browsing history, and it does not read pages you don't ask it to save.
  • It does not sell, share or send data to anyone, and it contains no remote code.
  • It never asks for or sees your passwords.

Permissions

  • Context menus: the right-click “Save to Grails” menu.
  • Storage: the connection code and recent items, above.
  • Active tab and scripting: to read the page or picture you chose to save.
  • Alarms: to try connecting to Grails again every half minute until it answers.
  • 127.0.0.1: to talk to the Grails app on your Mac.
  • All websites (content script): so holding ⌥ and clicking an image works on any page, and so a Pinterest board you open for import can be read.
